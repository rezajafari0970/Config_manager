## Identity and cache

`egress_unique` persists one immutable raw representative for each collector fingerprint. The existing fingerprint includes credentials, endpoint, routing and transport; it normalizes cosmetic remarks and supported encoding differences. It is not an endpoint-only or ASN identity. The raw bytes are retained unchanged and receipts remain bound to those exact bytes. A different label/source does not produce a second test or copied proof. A real identity change queues a new input. On disappearance, an unaccepted identity leaves the active inventory; its cache survives reappearance. Staged/archived bytes remain storage, never a queue entry before committed current membership.

`egress_inventory` is the shared current-identity plus retained-positive/IR-followup set. `egress_work` admits untested inputs, one bounded deep follow-up for an initial UNKNOWN, verified-positive renewal and sticky Iran confirmation. Expired work leases recover; raw metadata alone does not authorize a new run. Membership is checked again at renew/commit so disappearance cannot publish a new unretained result.

Verified positives are retained outside ordinary source expiry and keep the existing advance ten-minute Iran/route/health schedule. An IR quarantine follows its independent spaced confirmation rule. Both are necessary safety renewals, not repeated initial detection. No endless scans of29k archived raw representations. No runtime AI dependency.
